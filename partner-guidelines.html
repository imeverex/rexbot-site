Partner Guidelines

Effective November 4, 2026

RexBot community partners are streamers who use RexBot and promote it on their channel, in exchange for promotion from RexBot. These guidelines explain how that works. By applying, you confirm that you have read them and agree to follow them.

1. What the partnership is

A simple promotion exchange. No money changes hands, and there is no exclusivity: you are free to use and partner with other tools and services. A partnership is not employment, sponsorship or an endorsement of anything beyond RexBot itself.

2. Who can apply

  • Your Twitch account is at least 30 days old.
  • You have used RexBot for at least 14 days, with 2 or more RexBot features live on your stream.
  • You stream at least 3 times a week and have an established, active audience.
  • Your Twitch account is in good standing.

Applying does not guarantee a place. Applications are reviewed by hand, and we may waitlist or decline for any reason, including how many partners we can feature at once. If your application is not approved you can apply again after 30 days.

3. What partners do

  • An on-screen promo. Either a discreet static RexBot logo, or a short appearance of about 10 seconds roughly every 30 minutes. You choose which.
  • A light chat promo. One RexBot message about every 60 minutes while you are live, with a chat-activity minimum so it never fires into a quiet chat, plus a !rexbot command.
  • Use RexBot for real. Use the RexBot tools that fit your stream, so the promotion reflects how you actually use it.

4. What RexBot does

  • A featured website profile. Your channel image, a short bio and a watch link on rexbotapp.com, plus a place in the rotating homepage feature.
  • Social spotlights. A welcome post when you join, and one partner spotlight each month while you are active, using images or clips we agree with you and linking to your channel.
  • Help getting set up. Personal help choosing and setting up the RexBot features you want.

5. Conduct

  • Follow Twitch's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines at all times.
  • Do not use viewer-botting, follow-botting or any other fake engagement.
  • Be honest in your application and about your channel and audience.
  • Do not use the RexBot name or logo in a way that suggests more than the partnership described here.

6. Trial and reviews

Every partnership starts with a 60-day trial. We check in around day 30 and review at day 60, and the outcome is one of: keep going, extend the trial, or part ways. After that we review about every 90 days. At each review we check that your promo is running, that you still meet the requirements above, that you are following the conduct rules, and that RexBot has delivered its side.

7. Ending a partnership

Either side can end the partnership with 24 hours' notice, for any reason. We may also end it straight away, and remove your profile and promotion, if:

  • you break Twitch's rules, or the conduct rules above;
  • your promo is not running two weeks after we remind you;
  • you have not streamed for 30 days;
  • you fall below the requirements at two reviews in a row; or
  • you gave us false information.

When a partnership ends we take down your website profile and stop the social promotion. You remove the RexBot promo from your stream.

8. Your application

When you apply we keep your Twitch channel, the numbers and answers you enter, and the result of our checks against the requirements. The RexBot team reviews them in an admin dashboard, and a copy of the application is posted to a private Discord channel that only the review team can see. We use them only to decide on your application and to run the partnership. To have your application deleted, email support@rexbotapp.com. See also our Privacy Policy.

9. Changes

We may update these guidelines and will change the effective date when we do. If you are a current partner we will tell you about material changes, and you can end the partnership with 24 hours' notice if you do not agree with them.
