RexMusicBot
● Free · Discord bot

Play music in your Discord voice channel, on request.

One bot, one command. /play a song and it joins your voice channel and plays it.

Add to Discord
You'll pick which server to add it to on Discord's own page — needs "Manage Server" permission there.

Commands

Slash commands — type / in any channel it's in.

/playSearch + play a song, or queue it if something's playing
/skipSkip the current song
/pausePause playback
/resumeResume playback
/queueSee what's playing and what's next
/leaveDisconnect and clear the queue

Free, no catches

No premium tier, no paywalled commands — just /play and go.

Just search & go

Type a song, artist, or paste a link — no accounts or playlists to set up first.

Leaves you alone

Auto-disconnects after 5 idle minutes with nothing queued — doesn't linger in your voice channel.
