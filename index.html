What it does

Everything below is managed from your dashboard once you connect — no config files.

Custom commands

Add !discord, !socials, whatever you want. Cooldowns and permission levels built in.

Timers & auto-messages

Auto-post reminders on a repeating interval. Set it once, forget about it.

Moderation

Banned-word and link filtering with automatic timeouts, once the bot's a mod in your channel.

Song requests

!sr <song> queues straight to your Spotify. Needs Spotify Premium, same as any request bot.

Commands

Slash commands: type / in any channel it's in.

/playSearch + play a song, or queue it if something's playing
/skipSkip the current song
/pausePause playback
/resumeResume playback
/queueSee what's playing and what's next
/leaveDisconnect and clear the queue

Why it's simple

Free, no catches

No premium tier, no paywalled commands, just /play and go.

Just search & go

Type a song, artist, or paste a link. No accounts or playlists to set up first.

Leaves you alone

Auto-disconnects after 5 idle minutes with nothing queued, so it doesn't linger in your voice channel.
