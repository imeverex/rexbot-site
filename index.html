About

How Rex happened

I'm Everex. Competitive FPS player first, art and design nerd a close second. I studied computer science and new media production, so building my own tools was always going to happen eventually.

Here's what tipped it. To get every feature I wanted across Twitch and Discord, I was running three or four different bots. Different dashboards, different settings, and they all wanted to talk in chat at once. It drove me nuts. So I built one bot that does it all, and called him Rex.

His personality came from the best testing ground I had: my friends' streams. I'd jump into their chats on the Rex account and troll until he felt like a real character. Sorry, friends.

Rex is for everyone, whether you've been streaming for years or you went live for the first time last night. Setting him up should be easy, and you shouldn't need a guide to find your way around. And he's free. That part isn't changing.
